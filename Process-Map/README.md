# Hydrostatic Testing of LPG Cylinders – Process Map

## Overview
This process map outlines the hydrostatic testing workflow for LPG cylinders. It is designed to ensure structural integrity, safety compliance, and adherence to quality and regulatory standards.

## Purpose
To define the step-by-step procedure for hydrostatic pressure testing of LPG cylinders, ensuring that each unit meets required safety thresholds before being returned to service.

## Scope
Applies to LPG cylinder inspection, filling, and maintenance operations involving hydrostatic testing as part of routine safety assurance.

## Process Description
The process includes:
- Cylinder receipt and identification
- Visual inspection prior to testing
- Filling with water and air removal
- Application of test pressure using calibrated equipment
- Hold period for pressure stability observation
- Leak detection and deformation check
- Pressure release and draining
- Drying and final inspection
- Approval or rejection tagging

## Key Control Points
- Pre-test visual inspection
- Calibrated pressure equipment verification
- Safety exclusion zone enforcement
- Pressure hold monitoring
- Post-test integrity validation

## Safety Considerations
- High-pressure hazard present during testing
- Only certified personnel permitted to operate equipment
- PPE required at all stages
- Strict adherence to pressure limits and testing standards
